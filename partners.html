How we protect the brand

We hold MAP, and we watch it

We list at MAP and stay there. Where you don’t have a MAP policy, we follow your pricing guidance. Racing to the bottom burns your brand equity and our margin in the same move, so we don’t start it and we don’t match it.

We tell you who’s diverting

We watch the listings we sell on. When unauthorized sellers or grey-market product show up, you hear about it from us.

No channel conflict

We sell where you authorize us to sell, we tell you exactly where that is, and we don’t quietly open a new channel and let you find out later.

Your content, unchanged

We use your approved titles, images, and copy. We don’t write our own claims, don’t upload low-resolution images, and don’t leave your detail page worse than we found it.

Real stock, not a listing

Every unit we sell was bought from you and sits in our inventory. We don’t drop-ship your product from a third party and we don’t list what we can’t ship today.

We take the customer, and the returns

Questions, complaints, damages, and returns come to us, not to your support inbox. The review your product gets is on us to earn.

Clean chain of custody

We buy from you or from a distributor you’ve authorized. Nothing unauthorized, ever.

What brands usually ask

How big is the first PO?
Up to $10,000, sized to your MOQ and case pack. If the product moves, the second PO is bigger. We’d rather start honest and grow than open big and stall.
How fast do you reorder?
Usually monthly once a SKU is established, forecast against sell-through and your lead time. We reorder before we run out, not after.
Do you need terms?
No. We pay the PO in full before it ships. Nothing to underwrite, nothing to collect.
What do you need from us to start?
A wholesale price list, your product content and images, and your MAP and channel policies. After that we handle listing, pricing, inventory planning, and the customer.
What happens to our detail pages?
Nothing. We sell on your existing listings with your approved content. We don’t create duplicate listings and we don’t rewrite your detail pages.
Someone’s already diverting our product. Can you help?
We can give you visibility. We watch the listings we sell on and report unauthorized sellers and grey-market activity when we spot it. Enforcement is your call and your counsel’s, but you’ll know what’s happening.
Will you resell to other sellers?
No. We sell to end customers on the channels you authorize. We don’t wholesale your product onward and we don’t feed other resellers.
