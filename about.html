About Onuamo

Onuamo LLC is a family-owned online retailer, selling Health & Household, Home & Kitchen, and Grocery & Gourmet Food since 2021.

We work with brands growing their online business. Usually that means one of two situations: you’re moving into ecommerce properly for the first time, or you’re already there and tired of resellers who buy once, undercut everyone, and disappear.

We buy direct at wholesale and take title to the goods. Once your product is ours, how it’s listed, priced, and supported is our responsibility, and your brand is what’s on the line every time a customer opens the box.

You work with the owners. Three people run sourcing, operations, and marketing, and the person who answers your email is the person who signs the PO.
