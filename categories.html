What we buy

Three categories, on purpose. Narrow means we know the price ladders, the reorder rhythms, and what a good month looks like before we place the first PO.

Health & Household

Supplements, personal care, and household essentials. Repeat-purchase goods where going out of stock costs you the customer, not just the sale. We plan reorders against sell-through so that doesn’t happen.

Home & Kitchen

Kitchen tools, storage, organization, and durable household goods. Strong fit for brands whose product is genuinely better than the private-label version and needs the listing to prove it.

Grocery & Gourmet Food

Specialty foods, pantry staples, beverages, and organic products. We handle what the category demands: date-code and lot tracking, FIFO rotation, and the channel approvals that come with selling food online.
